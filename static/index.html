FAQs

How does the SOS button work?
Press and hold the SOS button for 3 seconds. It instantly alerts local police, the tourist helpline, and your emergency contacts with your live GPS location.
Is my passport data secure?
Yes. Your Digital Tourist ID is encrypted and stored securely. Only masked numbers are shown, and full data is shared only with verified authorities.
Can I use the app offline?
Your Digital ID and saved safe places work offline. SOS requires a network or SMS fallback to reach responders.
How do I add emergency contacts?
Go to Profile → Emergency Contacts, or tap the yellow "+" on the Safety Score card on the Home screen.
What is the Safety Score?
It's a live 0–100 rating of your surroundings based on crowd density, zone safety, time of day, and recent incident reports nearby. Higher is safer.
Which languages are supported?
The app and AI assistant support English, Hindi, German, French, Japanese, and Spanish. Change it anytime in Settings → Language.

Still need help?

Our support team is available 24×7 for travellers.
